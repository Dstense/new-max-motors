GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before
you take a look.

Start with the basics, then ask us about the specific car you have in mind.

Are the featured cars still available?+

Online listings can change or expire. Contact NEW MAX MOTORS on WhatsApp to confirm availability, current kilometres and the final price before visiting.

Can I sell my car to NEW MAX MOTORS?+

Use the sell/exchange enquiry form to share your car's model, year, kilometres, fuel type and expected price. The details will be prepared in a WhatsApp message for you to review and send.

Can I ask about a car that is not shown here?+

Yes. Send your preferred make, model, budget and transmission preference, and ask about the latest stock.

What should I check before buying a used car?+

Verify the registration certificate, insurance, loan or hypothecation status, service records, accident history, challans, ownership details and vehicle condition. Consider an independent inspection and a test drive before paying.
